Nguyen Xuan Phuc

I'm Phuc, and I'm passionate about web development because I enjoy turning my own ideas into functional websites and applications. What I particularly like about it is the combination of creative design, logical thinking, and solving real problems. Currently, I mainly work with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, as well as Git and GitHub. I learn mostly independently and try to apply new knowledge directly to my own projects. It's important to me not only to understand how something works, but also to become increasingly independent by experimenting and solving problems on my own. At the moment, I'm primarily deepening my knowledge of JavaScript. As my next major step, I want to learn React and modern frontend development, with the long-term goal of developing my skills toward full-stack development. Outside of web development, I'm also interested in personal growth. Strength training, reading, and continuously learning new skills are important parts of my life as well. In general, I like the idea of improving step by step and using what I learn to create something of my own.
